What this is

Dance Platform is a personal project that catalogues dance tutorials published on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram, and gives you tools to practise with them — loops, speed control, timestamps, notes and a practice log.

The videos are not ours

Every video is embedded from the platform it was published on, and plays from there. We host no video. Credit and rights stay with the people who made them, and their platform's terms apply while you watch. If you are a creator and want a video off the site, say so through the feedback button in the corner of any page and it will be removed.

Your account

Keep your password to yourself, and give an email address that reaches you — it is the only way to get back into your account if you forget the password. You can delete your account at any time from your profile, and everything personal goes with it.

What you add

You can add dances and videos. Anything you add may be reviewed before it becomes visible to other people, and anything that is spam, abusive, or not a dance video will be removed along with, if it keeps happening, the account that posted it. Don't add material you have no right to link to.

No guarantees

This runs on a single small machine, as a hobby. It may be slow, it may be down, and data may occasionally be lost despite nightly backups. Learn to dance at your own risk: warm up, and stop if something hurts.

Privacy

Last updated {{ updated }}

What is stored

If you make an account: your username, name, optional nickname, email address, an optional avatar URL, and a hash of your password — never the password itself. Then whatever you do here: favourites, what you've learned or are working on, ratings, video notes, loops, your practice log, and anything you add to the catalogue.

You can browse the whole catalogue without an account, and nothing about you is stored if you do.

What it is used for

Running the site, and nothing else. There is no advertising, no analytics service, no tracking pixels, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone. The single email this site can send you is a password reset link, and only when you ask for one.

Who can see it

Your profile is private by default. Setting it to public shares your name, nickname, avatar, the dances you've learned and any skill trees you've chosen to share — nothing else, and never your email address or practice log.

Other people's players

Videos play in embedded YouTube, TikTok and Instagram players. Those platforms set their own cookies and see their own requests when a video loads; that part is governed by their privacy policies, not this one. This site itself stores only what it needs in your browser: your sign-in token and a few display preferences.

Deleting everything

Profile → Account → Delete account removes your account and everything personal attached to it — practice log, notes, loops, favourites, ratings, personal videos and skill trees — immediately and permanently. Dances you added to the shared catalogue stay, with your name detached from them. Copies can survive in the nightly database backups for a few months before those roll off.

Getting in touch

This is a one-person project; the fastest route is the feedback button in the corner of any page. If you want your data removed and can't sign in, use it to say so.
